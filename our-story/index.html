Our Story

Three decades of turning crisis into clarity

TRS has built its reputation among disaster recovery and claims professionals by breaking down intricate recovery challenges into understandable components for all involved parties.

Founder & Leadership

Michael Mies serves as founder and President of Technical Recovery Solutions (TRS). Over three decades, Mike has managed thousands of projects globally, addressing equipment affected by both natural and man-made disasters. He established himself early in the restoration sector as an equipment recovery specialist and has maintained this core focus throughout his career.

— Michael Mies, Founder & President
